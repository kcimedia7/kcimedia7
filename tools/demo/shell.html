SplatWorks SplatWorks Photos and video to gaussian splats, in your browser Preview backend

This runs entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Because a browser can't run COLMAP or a CUDA trainer, it uses the project's preview reconstruction: it assumes you orbited the subject and back-projects each frame into gaussians. That's a fast proxy, not structure-from-motion — real pose solving and .ply export are in the local app.

Drop a video or a set of photos

Best results come from a full orbit around one subject — 20–60 stills, or a slow 10–30 second walk-around clip. Frames are decoded by your browser, so any format it can play will work.
